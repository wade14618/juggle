How to play

Concept

The ring is a juggler's clock. Each spot is one beat of time, counting counterclockwise and repeating.

Placing a number on a spot means you toss a ball on that beat. The ball stays in the air for that number of beats, and its arrow points to the beat it lands on.

The 2 at the top stays up for two beats.
Every beat catches one ball.

Rules

  • Place one number on every spot.
  • Each ball must come down on a beat of its own. Two arrows into one spot means two balls land together, and one is dropped.
  • Ringed numbers are fixed.

Controls

  • Drag a number onto a spot, or tap the number, then the spot.
  • Drag a placed number to another spot to move it.
  • Tap a placed number, or drag it off the ring, to remove it.
  • Hint places one correct number.

Why "Juggle"?

This is how jugglers write down tricks. Every puzzle you solve is a real juggling pattern, and you'll see it juggled when you win.

How to play

  1. Every number is a jump. A 3 jumps three spots clockwise. Jumps wrap around the ring.
  2. Tap a number, then tap an empty spot to put it there. A line shows where it lands.
  3. Fill the ring so that every spot is landed on exactly once.
  4. Numbers with a ring around them are already set and cannot move.

In the picture, the 2 at the top jumps two spots. Follow each arrow: no spot has two arrows coming in, and none is missed.

Controls

Tap a number you placed to take it back. Hint places one correct number. Undo takes back your last move. Reset clears your numbers.

Tips

A number as big as the ring jumps all the way round and lands on its own spot. If two arrows hit one spot, both turn white and dotted, and the spot gets a white ring.

Why "Juggle"?

This is how jugglers write down their tricks. Each number is how many beats a ball stays in the air, and a trick only works if no two balls come down on the same beat. Every puzzle you solve here is a real juggling pattern.
